# The warm-up library picker (mockups, waiting for a pick)

The library and the check-in rule shipped in v1.111.0. These are the screens for browsing it. Live app on demo data, dark and light, 390 px.

1. **The day's warm-up** (`1-today`): This day / Every session, as now. "For today" holds the check-in's one or two suggestions, with why ("Your hips were stiffer than usual at the check-in"), Add and Skip today. Your warm-up stays editable (arrows, remove). Add from the warm-up library opens the library.
2. **The library** (`2-library`): two ways in, By body part (shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, back, wrists) and What feels stiff, each item with its dose and evidence ("backed by research", "some research support", "coaching consensus"). Anything that loads a joint you're working around is marked, never hidden.

In v1.111.0 the suggestions already appear in the day's warm-up list, as items with their reason. What's new here is For today as its own group with Skip, and the library to browse.
