# The plan for now: setup flow and place editor (mockups, waiting for a pick)

These replace Part 4 screens 4 (hold on Today) and 5 (the hold offer). Live app screens on demo data, dark and light, 390 px. The engine shipped in v1.112.0; nothing on screen starts a plan until these are picked.

1. **The offer** on Today (`1-offer`): "3 of your 10 planned sessions in the last two weeks", what a plan for now is, and Set up a plan for now / Not now. **The way in from Edit program** (`1b-edit-program`): Train differently for a while.
2. **Places** (`2-places`, step 1 of 4): each place with what it has, and days a week at each (− / +). Add a place opens the editor.
3. **The place editor** (`3-place-editor`): a name, what it has (the same equipment list as setup), and noise (anything goes, or quiet: no jumping or dropped weights).
4. **How long, and until when** (`4-length-until`): session length (Full, 45, 30, 20) and an end date, or Not sure.
5. **The week it builds** (`5-week`): each day, where, and its exercises. The gym day has the main lifts and what needs the gym; home days have the rest. Below that, each muscle's weekly sets. **Very little training** (`5b-week-low`): one short day a week. It says the plan keeps each muscle at the research's minimum for keeping size: expect to keep what you have, not to grow.
6. **Today on the plan** (`6-today`): where you are (week 3, until 15 January, the program waiting at its week), Change the plan, Back to my program, and the six-week question asked once (Keep going, Change it, Make it my program).
7. **The offer to go back** (`7-back`): after a full week of the program's sessions at the gym (or on the end date). Each lift starts from its own last session; one away for 3+ weeks starts a little lighter.

Step 4 of 4 would be a confirmation ("Start the plan"), the same as setup's last step; not drawn.
