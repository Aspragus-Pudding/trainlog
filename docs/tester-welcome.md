# Tester welcome message

Send this with each tester's own setup link (their backup sheet and notes
address are in the link, so send it to them only, never to a group).

---

Hi, and thanks for testing Trainlog.

It's a strength training app that builds your program, suggests the weight and
reps for every set, and adjusts as you log. It's an early test version, so
things will change and some will break. Your notes are the most useful part.

**Install it**
1. On iPhone, open this link in Safari: [your setup link]
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the app from the new icon.
   On Android, open the link in Chrome and tap Install app.
3. Go through setup (a few minutes; skip anything you don't know). You'll see
   your whole plan before anything is saved.
4. Then open **Settings** (last tab) → You and your sheet → **Setup link**,
   paste the same link and tap **Apply**. That connects your notes.

**What to expect**
- Before each workout, a 30-second check-in. The app adjusts the day from it.
- Amber numbers are suggestions. Lift what you actually can and log it
  honestly, including how hard it was (RPE: 10 = nothing left, 8 = about two
  reps left). The app learns from that.
- Short cards explain things the first time they happen. The ? on each screen
  gives a tour.
- Your log stays on your phone. After your first session, save a backup when
  it asks (Settings → Save backup).

**Sending notes**
Tap the pencil at the top of any screen. Say what you were doing, what you
expected, and what happened. It records the screen and app version for you.
Small things count too: anything confusing, awkward or wrong.

Thanks again. Message me if anything stops you training.
