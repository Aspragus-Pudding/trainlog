# Trainlog — for testers

Trainlog builds a strength program for you, suggests the weight and reps for
every set, and adjusts as you log. Thanks for trying it. Your notes are the
point of this test, so send them freely, even small ones.

## Install it (iPhone)

1. Open the link you were sent in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open the app from the new
   icon.
3. Choose **Build it together** (recommended) or **Build it for me**, and
   answer the questions. It takes a few minutes, and you can skip anything you
   don't know. The header shows how far along you are ("Step 3 of 9"). You see
   the whole plan, with a reason for each part, before anything is saved.
4. Six short cards explain how your program works, using your own plan. Read
   them or tap **Skip and start**. You can replay them later from **Settings →
   Help**.
5. In the app, open **Settings** (the last tab), go to **You and your sheet**,
   paste the same link under **Setup link** and tap **Apply**. This connects
   your notes (and your backup sheet, if you were given one). It's needed
   because the home-screen app doesn't share Safari's storage.

On Android, open the link in Chrome and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home
screen**, then follow steps 3 to 5.

## Your first session

- The first time something happens (your first check-in, a suggestion that
  moved, an all-out set, a deload), a short card says what it is and why.
  **Got it** puts it away; **More** opens the guide.
- Before you start, the **Workout** tab is today's workout. Move, swap, add or
  remove exercises and change sets there. Changes are for today unless you tap
  **Save to program**.
- The **check-in** asks how you slept and how you feel. 3 means normal; only
  low answers change anything.
- **Amber numbers are suggestions.** Change the weight and the reps
  recompute, or the other way round.
- **RPE** is how hard the set was: 10 means nothing left, 8 means
  about two reps left. Be honest; the next set is priced from it. If you'd
  rather the app said "effort", change it in **Settings → Words and units**.
- Your very first set of an exercise has no suggestion unless you entered a
  recent set in setup. Pick something you can do for the target reps, and the
  app takes it from there.
- If you've picked something to work around, a flagged exercise shows a **For
  your shoulder** chip (or knee, elbow…). Tap it for what to watch and a swap.
- After your first session the app offers to save a backup. Say yes. Your
  log lives only on this phone until you do.
- The **?** at the top of any screen walks you through that screen. **End
  tour** stops it at any step.

## Leaving notes

Tap the **pencil** at the top of any screen. The most useful notes say what
you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Pick a kind
(Something broke, Annoying / confusing, or Idea).

Notes go to the person who gave you the app, with your name and the screen
you were on. If you're offline they're kept and sent later. You can see what
you've sent under **Progress → Notes**.

**If your notes don't seem to arrive:** you probably haven't pasted the setup
link (step 5 above). In the meantime, **Progress → Notes → Copy all notes**,
then paste them into a message.

## Your data

- Your log stays on your phone. Nobody else can see it unless you set up a
  backup sheet, and then only the person who made that sheet.
- **Back it up.** If you were given a backup sheet, it updates after every
  session. If not, use **Settings → Save backup** now and then and keep the
  file in Files. The app reminds you if it's been over a week.
- **Restore** from **Settings → Restore from backup**. It checks the file
  first, and restoring the same file twice changes nothing.

## Updates

- New versions arrive on their own. When one is ready, a bar at the bottom
  says so. Tap it when you're ready. It never switches mid-workout.
- A short "What's new" card on the dashboard tells you what changed, and
  **Settings → About** lists the recent versions.
- The version number is in **Settings → About**. Please include it if you're
  reporting a bug (notes include it automatically).
- An update never changes or deletes anything you've logged.

## If something goes wrong

Your logged data is safe. If a screen shows "Something went wrong", tap
**Back to dashboard** and carry on, then leave a note saying what you were
doing.

- **If something on your phone looks wrong**, such as a button you can't
  reach, open **Settings → About** and tap **Diagnostics** (or long-press the
  version number). Tap **Copy**, and paste it into a note. It says which
  phone, browser and screen size you have.
- **If setup closes or you leave it**, your answers are kept. Open the app
  and tap **Continue setup**.
- **The app also notes its own errors** under **Progress → Notes → App
  errors**. They stay on your phone unless you tap **Send to the developer**.
