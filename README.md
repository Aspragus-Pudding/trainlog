# Trainlog — for testers

Trainlog builds a strength program for you, suggests the weight and reps for
every set, and adjusts as you log. Thanks for trying it. Your notes are the
point of this test, so send them freely, even small ones.

## Install it (iPhone)

1. Open the link you were sent in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open the app from the new
   icon.
3. Go through the setup questions. It takes about three minutes, and you can
   skip anything you don't know.
4. In the app, go to **Edit program**, scroll to **Setup link**, paste the same
   link, tap **Apply**, then **Save**. This connects your notes (and your
   backup sheet, if you were given one). It's needed because the home-screen
   app doesn't share Safari's storage.

## Your first session

- Short hints point at things the first time they appear. Tap **Got it**, or
  tap anywhere else to close one. They never cover the Log set button.
- Before each workout, the **check-in** asks how you slept and how you feel.
  3 means normal; only low answers change anything.
- **Amber numbers are suggestions.** Change the weight and the reps
  recompute, or the other way round.
- **Effort (RPE)** is how hard the set was: 10 means nothing left, 8 means
  about two reps left. Be honest; the next set is priced from it.
- Your very first set of an exercise has no suggestion. Pick something you
  can do for the target reps, and the app takes it from there.
- The **?** at the top of any screen walks you through that screen.

## Leaving notes

Tap the **pencil** at the top of any screen. The most useful notes say what
you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Pick a kind
(Something broke, Annoying / confusing, or Idea).

Notes go to the person who gave you the app, with your name and the screen
you were on. If you're offline they're kept and sent later. You can see what
you've sent under **Progress → Notes**.

**If your notes don't seem to arrive:** you probably haven't pasted the setup
link (step 4 above). In the meantime, **Progress → Notes → Copy all notes**,
then paste them into a message.

## Your data

- Your log stays on your phone. Nobody else can see it unless you set up a
  backup sheet, and then only the person who made that sheet.
- **Back it up.** If you were given a backup sheet, it updates after every
  session. If not, use **Lifts → Save backup** now and then and keep the file
  in Files. The app reminds you if it's been over a week.
- **Restore** from **Lifts → Restore from backup**. It checks the file first,
  and restoring the same file twice changes nothing.

## Updates

- New versions arrive on their own. When one is ready, a bar at the bottom
  says so. Tap it when you're ready. It never switches mid-workout.
- A short "What's new" card on the dashboard tells you what changed.
- The version number is on the **Lifts** tab, under Your data. Please include
  it if you're reporting a bug (notes include it automatically).
- An update never changes or deletes anything you've logged.

## If something goes wrong

Your logged data is safe. If a screen shows "Something went wrong", tap
**Back to dashboard** and carry on, then leave a note saying what you were
doing.
